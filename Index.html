About Us

Who we are and what we do

Our Story

EasyLearn started as a small project by a group of teachers and students who wanted good study material in one place. Many learners find online lessons too fast or too complicated. We write ours in plain language and add examples.

Today the website offers courses, lessons, tutorials and downloadable resources for school and university students, and for anyone who wants to learn a new skill.

Our Mission

To make learning simple and open to everyone who has a phone or a computer.

Our Vision

A world where no student is stopped from learning by cost or distance.

Our Values

Clear teaching, honesty, respect for learners and continuous improvement.

Lessons

Learning materials, topic by topic

Click a lesson title to open it. Each lesson takes about 10 to 20 minutes.

Computer Basics

Lesson 1: Parts of a computer

A computer has input devices (keyboard, mouse), a processor, memory, storage and output devices (screen, printer). In this lesson you learn what each part does and how they work together.

Lesson 2: Using the keyboard and mouse

Learn the main keys, how to click and double-click, and useful shortcuts such as Ctrl+C to copy and Ctrl+V to paste.

Lesson 3: Files and folders

Create, rename, move and delete files. Keep your documents organized so you can find them quickly.

English for Study

Lesson 1: Nouns, verbs and adjectives

The basic types of words, with simple examples and short exercises.

Lesson 2: Writing a good paragraph

Start with a main idea, add supporting sentences and end with a closing sentence.

Lesson 3: Reading for meaning

Find the main idea, guess new words from context and answer questions about a text.

Mathematics Foundations

Lesson 1: Fractions and decimals

Add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions, and change them to decimals and percentages.

Lesson 2: Introduction to algebra

Use letters for unknown numbers and solve simple equations step by step.

Tutorials

Step-by-step guides you can follow

How to Create Your First Web Page

  1. Open Notepad (or any text editor) and create a new file.
  2. Type the basic HTML structure with html, head and body tags.
  3. Add a heading and a paragraph inside the body.
  4. Save the file as index.html.
  5. Double-click the file to open it in your browser.

How to Write a CV

  1. Add your name and contact details.
  2. Write a short summary.
  3. List education and experience.
  4. Add your skills.
  5. Check spelling and save as PDF.

How to Make a Study Timetable

  1. List all your subjects.
  2. Mark the hours you are free.
  3. Give more time to hard subjects.
  4. Add short breaks.
  5. Follow it for one week and adjust.

How to Send an Email

  1. Sign in to your email account.
  2. Click Compose.
  3. Enter the address and subject.
  4. Write your message.
  5. Click Send.
